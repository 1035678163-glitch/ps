# Looks — presets, references, stocks, and clichés to avoid

Load this at workflow step 2, before writing prose. Picking a look decides blocks 3 (Light), 4 (Camera) and 10 (Palette/grade) in one move, and — more importantly — keeps them consistent with each other. Inconsistency between those three is what makes an image feel assembled rather than photographed.

## 2. Reference library

Two columns on purpose. **Prefer the attributes column.** Some providers filter or ignore style-mimicry of living artists, the names are hit-or-miss across models, and the attributes are what actually change the pixels. Use a name only as a shortcut when you are confident the model knows it — and always keep the attributes in the prompt as well, so the frame survives if the name is dropped.

## 4. Clichés to avoid

The skill's negative list guards against *CGI* tells. This list guards against *AI-beautiful* tells, which is now the more common failure. Add the relevant ones to block 10's negative list when the scene invites them.

**Lighting** — dual rim lights with no motivating source · lens flare with no source in frame · god rays in every interior · dust motes in every beam (this skill's own advice, now a cliché — use where air would actually carry dust) · perfect golden hour on every exterior · wet ground with no rain.

**Color** — teal-and-orange applied by reflex · every shadow pushed blue · uniform high saturation · the "cinematic" crushed-black-lifted-shadow LUT on subjects that don't want it.

**Composition** — dead-center horizon · perfect bilateral symmetry where none is motivated · subject facing camera and smiling · hero pose, low angle, backlit · everything in frame relevant to the subject · unresolved tangents at the frame edge.

**Faces** — the AI composite face (symmetrical, poreless, mid-twenties, ambiguous ethnicity) · both eyes identically catchlit · a smile with no eye involvement · flawless teeth · hands at rest and empty · no one blinking, ever.

**Rendering** — uniform sharpness across every plane · perfectly circular bokeh balls · micro-detail everywhere at the same scale · HDR halos on every edge · noise-free shadows.

**The strongest single antidote:** ask what an ordinary photograph of this scene would contain that a beautiful one would leave out — then put one of those things back in.
